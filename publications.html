Publications

My research develops execution-grounded AI for scientific and computational systems, with AI for Science and AI for Systems tied together by validation from compilation, profiling, runtime behavior, biological structure, measurement, and scientific computing. This page groups the publication record into execution-grounded AI for code and systems; AI for biology and RNA editing; AI for materials, microscopy, and scientific imaging; and scientific computing, HPC, and distributed foundations.
